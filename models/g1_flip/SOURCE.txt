Whole-body tracking policy and the flip_02 clip.

The policy was trained in wbc-mjlab on LAFAN1 plus a small set of BONES-SEED flips.
flip_02 is the flip clip that repository enables by default.
Action is a residual on the clip joint pose. Control rate is 50 Hz.
